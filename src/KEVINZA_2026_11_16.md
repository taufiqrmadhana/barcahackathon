## Reproduction of the README scores

An isolated archive of `a0cdd64` ran `python src/run.py --out results`, using its
default parameters and the complete raw `film_5hz` observations under its expected
`film` filename. This cache has 2,221 samples and 1,501 native public training rows;
its SHA-256 is recorded in the aggregate report. The main checkout stayed on
`taufiq`. No prediction rows were copied into the Taufiq pipeline.

The standalone run took 60 seconds and reproduced both README numbers:

| What was measured | Public macroF1 | Public spellF1 | Possessions |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Branch's printed OOF check | 0.8282 | 0.6118 | 17 |
| Branch's written `barca.csv` | 0.986820 | 0.912500 | 15 |

The written CSV uses `Layer.proba(..., oof=False)`, including inside the public
training window. Its classifier has seen those public labels. The printed OOF
check is separate: it uses a 50-frame purge and stitches probabilities before
decoding. The Taufiq video validation uses a 125-frame purge and independently
decodes each whole half with the corresponding held-out model before stitching
its public slice. Both pipelines also have different physical baselines.

Public supervision is allowed by the challenge. The written CSV is a valid
submission output; its public training-window score measures a different thing
from held-out validation. Neither number establishes private performance.

The earlier reproduction used the sparse original cache, with 1,257 samples and
537 public samples. That explains why it did not reproduce the README's 0.9125.
The new complete-cache run resolves that discrepancy; the old report remains a
record of the inputs actually available on November 15.

## Ideas tested in Taufiq's independent decoder

Kevinza's 500-tree ExtraTrees idea was also fitted on the native 5 Hz grid here,
with continuous/video, full-context and extra physical features, and leaf sizes
2 or 10. It did not improve the existing macro-v4 checkpoint. The strongest
spell result among these standalone tree trials was 0.744444, with macroF1
0.922697. Confidence fusion and the v2 blend had already failed the earlier audit.

The new `origin/neo/loop-pipeline` at `51e7833` supplied gap-kick, delayed contact
and noise-aware velocity ideas. None improved macro v4 after the existing video
head. A motion mixture with weight 0.65 reached 0.943537 / 0.800000, but regressed
at four folds; it was retained only as a local experiment.

An additional video-only experiment extracted ankle/pose evidence from 2,221
fused-ball crops with YOLO11n-pose. Small distant players and missed ankles limited
support. Logistic heads, confident expert gates and quality-filtered contact
overrides did not improve the checkpoint. Pose features are not a production input.

The successful change came from combining the macro and motion models at the
temporal level, with a separate average of their dead-ball probabilities. It
reaches **0.956130 macroF1 / 0.875000 spellF1** with independent held-out decoding.
See [MACRO_V5.md](MACRO_V5.md) for the algorithm, ablations and match statistics.

All method selection reuses the same 17 public possessions. Repartition checks
are sensitivity checks on that slice, not new independent test sets. Aggregate
trials and fingerprints are in `bench/results/macro_v5_2026-11-16.json`.
